01About

I care about how things actually work — and how to make them work better.

Notes on work, software and learning.

Industrial engineering taught me to look at work as a system: inputs, flows, bottlenecks and decisions. Software gives me a way to act on what I see — to build tools that remove manual steps, collect better data and support better decisions.

Most of what I build is in C# and .NET. Alongside that I'm learning Python, Microsoft Azure and AI, mainly through projects. I'm still early on this path, and I prefer to learn by making something real, then improving it.

How I approach a problem

  1. A

    Understand the process

    Map how things really work before trying to change them.

  2. B

    Build something that works

    A small, useful version first. Complexity can come later.

  3. C

    Measure, then improve

    Let data decide what to change next, then repeat.
